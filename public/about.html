About Income School

Income School is a free platform launched in 2024 to help Africans learn digital skills that generate real income.

Our Mission

Make quality digital skills education accessible to everyone in Africa, free of charge.

What We Offer

  • 16+ professional courses
  • Web Development (Full Stack)
  • Crypto & Trading Foundations
  • AI Integration for Apps
  • Freelance Copywriting
  • YouTube Automation & Growth
  • Digital Business & Monetization
  • And more...

Why Free?

We believe financial barriers shouldn't prevent anyone from learning valuable skills. Our platform is supported by ads so students learn for free.
