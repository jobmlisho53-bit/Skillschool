Freelancing in Kenya: Complete Beginner's Guide (2026)

Published: May 20, 2026

Why Freelancing in Kenya is Growing

With stable internet, M-Pesa payments, and global platforms, Kenyan freelancers are earning in dollars. Top freelancers make $2000-5000/month.

Best Freelance Platforms for Kenyans

  • Upwork - Highest rates ($20-100/hour)
  • Fiverr - Good for beginners ($10-50/gig)
  • Freelancer.ke - Local Kenyan jobs
  • Guru.com - Project-based work

Most In-Demand Skills

  • Web Development ($30-100/hour)
  • Copywriting ($25-75/hour)
  • Graphic Design ($20-50/hour)
  • Virtual Assistant ($10-25/hour)
  • Video Editing ($25-50/hour)

How to Get Your First Client

  1. Complete a free course on Income School
  2. Build 2-3 portfolio samples
  3. Create a professional profile
  4. Apply to 10 jobs daily
  5. Start at lower rates, raise after reviews

Getting Paid as a Kenyan Freelancer

  • Payoneer → M-Pesa (2-3 days)
  • Wise (TransferWise) → Bank (1-2 days)
  • Direct Wire Transfer → Bank (3-5 days)
  • PayPal → M-Pesa (via third-party services)
